---
name: qa
description: Ask focused clarifying questions before acting when the user's goal, scope, or desired output is unclear.
---

Use this skill when the user's goal, scope, constraints, or desired output is
unclear enough that acting immediately could lead to the wrong result.

- Ask one focused question at a time.
- Ask no more than three clarifying questions.
- Do not ask questions when a reasonable assumption is safe and useful.
- Stop asking once the task is clear enough to act.
